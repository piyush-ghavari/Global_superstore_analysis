# Global Superstore Data Analysis Dashboard

## Introduction
This project uses the **Global Superstore** dataset to provide insights into sales, profits, and customer behavior across different regions and product categories. 
The dashboard offers visualizations that help understand performance metrics and identify trends in the retail space.

# Project Tasks
- Sales Insights: Evaluate discount impact, shipping preferences, and loyal customers.
- Visualization: Create charts to highlight key insights.
- Recommendations: Suggest strategies based on findings to boost sales and customer engagement.


## Dataset
- **Source**: Global Superstore dataset (https://public.tableau.com/app/learn/sample-data)


## Setup and Installation
1. Install [Tableau](https://www.tableau.com) (if you haven't already).
2. Download the **Global Superstore** dataset.
3. Import the dataset into Tableau or any visualization tool you are using.

### Insights

1. **Order Priority and Profit**:  
   In the Global Superstore dataset, for all three categories (Office Supplies, Furniture, and Technology), **Medium priority** orders show **higher sales and profit**, while **Low** and **Critical** priority orders have **lower sales and profit** compared to Medium priority.

2. **Tables in Furniture Category**:  
   **Tables** in the Furniture category have a **negative profit margin**, indicating potential issues with pricing or operational costs.

3. **Ship Mode and Profit**:  
   In the Global Superstore dataset, **Standard Class** ship mode tends to have **higher profits**, while **First Class** and **Same Day** shipping options show **lower profit margins**, possibly due to higher shipping costs or premium service expenses.

---

### Recommendations

1. **Focus on Medium Priority Orders**:  
   Given that **Medium priority** orders consistently show higher sales and profit, it’s beneficial to prioritize these orders and streamline operations to handle them efficiently.

2. **Review Pricing and Costs for Furniture Tables**:  
   The **negative profit margin** for **Tables** in the Furniture category should be addressed by reviewing pricing strategies, production costs, and identifying any inefficiencies.

3. **Optimize Shipping Strategies**:  
   Since **Standard Class** shipping provides higher profits, consider offering incentives for customers to choose this shipping mode, or evaluate the cost-effectiveness of **First Class** and **Same Day** shipping options.
